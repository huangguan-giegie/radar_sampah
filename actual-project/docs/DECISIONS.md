# Team 04 Decisions

## 2026-08-13 — Project direction

- The vote favoured `Idea 1 Version 2` (4 votes) over `Idea 1` (2 votes).
- Working title: Marine Litter Hotspot & Marine-Life Observation Platform.
- Marine litter is the main reporting flow.
- Marine life is an OBIS map/context layer, not a second reporting flow.
- Freeze one Malaysian coastal area for the first MVP.
- Drop repeated ideas and avoid building multiple reporting flows in parallel.

## 2026-08-13 — Safety and scope

- Use public/synthetic data only.
- Do not expose exact threatened-species locations.
- Do not claim pollution-source proof, verified species identity or enforcement decisions.
- Start with transparent rules or a clearly labelled demo model.
