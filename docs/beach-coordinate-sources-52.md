# Sources for all 82 located Malaysia beach catalogue records

Prepared: **6 October 2026**. Scope: the supplied 82-row beach-name export.

The catalogue now contains **82 coordinate references for all 82 export rows**. The first research pass documented **52 accepted coordinate pairs**. A later supplement added **27 Google Maps references supplied in `beach_coordinates.csv`**, and a final Malaysia-only research pass added **3 additional references** for Landing Beach, Cenderawasih Beach and Batu village Beach. The four separate MVP beaches remain outside this document; including them, the application now has **86 located records out of 86**.

This document records the origin, precision and matching evidence for the full 82-row export. The original 52-entry register is retained below for audit continuity, followed by a 30-entry supplement covering the coordinates added on 6 October 2026. Approximate island-, resort-, beachfront- and triangulated references are explicitly labelled and are not represented as surveyed beach centroids.

## Precision and acceptance rules

- Coordinates are written as decimal latitude and longitude, in that order. The number of stored decimal places does not establish surveying accuracy.
- A named Google Maps entity was resolved through its public CID/place link or embedded place record. In a place URL, the `!3d` and `!4d` values identify the marker. The `/@latitude,longitude` viewport, general search-map centre and listing map centre were excluded as coordinate evidence.
- Named beach geometry can support a representative point within the mapped beach area. Published DMS values were converted to decimal degrees with their source resolution recorded. Government event locations and study sampling locations remain reference points with their specific meaning.
- Eight beachfront resort/property markers are accepted only as **approximate shore references**. They identify the associated beachfront property and do not measure the beach boundary. They are labelled in every affected row below.
- Island centres, city centres, offshore anchorages or dive points, unrelated same-name beaches and contradictory identities were not substituted for a beach coordinate.
- Coordinates add a location reference. They do not establish current biodiversity, pollution, public access, visiting safety or survey-quality beach boundaries.

The school Google Workspace account disables interactive Maps. Research used public place links and publicly accessible embedded records; the account and its settings were not changed. OpenStreetMap-derived geographic data is attributed to [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) under ODbL. No provider reviews, photographs or descriptive content were imported into the application.
